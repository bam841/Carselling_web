Flexible Financing Options

Choose the purchasing method that best aligns with your financial plan.

Hire Purchase (HP)

Spread the cost of your car over 12 to 60 fixed monthly payments. Once the final payment is cleared, you own the car 100%.

  • ✓ Fixed interest rate for entire term
  • ✓ No end-of-contract balloon payments
  • ✓ Low initial deposit options available

Part-Exchange Equity

Use your existing vehicle as the complete deposit on your next car. We handle all lender payoff settlements.

  • ✓ No out-of-pocket deposit needed
  • ✓ Settle existing finance seamlessly
  • ✓ Drive away the same afternoon

Direct Outright Purchase

Simple, transparent cash or direct bank transfer pricing with full warranty, inspection, and transfer assistance included.

  • ✓ Zero interest or finance charges
  • ✓ Immediate title transfer
  • ✓ 12 months' warranty included
