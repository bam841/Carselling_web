ABOUT MJCK MOTORS

A modern used car dealership built on trust and clarity

We founded MJCK Motors to eliminate the high-pressure sales pitches, hidden administrative surcharges, and uncertainty that too often accompany buying a pre-owned vehicle.

Quality inspected before it hits the showroom

Every vehicle in our showroom is selected through rigorous mechanical, electrical, and legal criteria. If a car doesn’t pass our comprehensive 150-point diagnostic inspection and complete maintenance audit, we simply do not sell it.

Our showroom advisors are salaried rather than commissioned, meaning our team is incentivized to find you the right car for your needs—not to push you into an unsuitable purchase.

Our Multi-Point Verification Standard

What goes into inspecting every car before it qualifies for the MJCK 12-month warranty.

Diagnostic ECU Scan

Complete computer system scan checking powertrain, ABS, airbags, transmission controllers, and emissions sensors for hidden error codes.

Undercarriage & Structural

Full hydraulic lift inspection verifying chassis straightness, suspension bushings, subframes, steering gear, brake rotors, and exhaust assembly.

Road Dynamics Test

Extended road testing evaluating transmission shifts, emergency braking response, wheel alignment, highway stability, and cabin acoustic insulation.
